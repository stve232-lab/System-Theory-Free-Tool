The simulation resamples your real trading days, so a bad day stays a bad day, but the order of days is shuffled and the future won't match the backtest exactly. It uses closed-trade P&L only; floating drawdown, slippage and spread widening make real results worse, so treat this as an upper bound. There is no time limit, as with most CFD challenges. Firm rules were checked in October 2026 and can change; verify them on the firm's site. Your report never leaves your browser. Not financial advice.
